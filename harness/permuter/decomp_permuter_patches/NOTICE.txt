Changes made for decomp-harness:
- compile_cmd.json next to a work unit's compile.sh may give the compiler argv
  directly ({"argv": [...]}); the permuter appends "-c SOURCE -o OUTPUT" and
  runs it without a shell. bash is then not required. Units that mark
  compile_cmd.json "supported": false keep using compile.sh.
- When no `cpp` is on PATH, base.c is read verbatim (correct for the harness's
  already-preprocessed work units) instead of failing.
